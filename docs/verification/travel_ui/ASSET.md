Ngày tạo: 11/11/2026. Dùng skill imagegen ở chế độ built-in, không dùng CLI/API key của dự án.

Wide 16:9 composition, pool and resort mostly in center and right, open darker foliage and sea on the left suitable for a readable text overlay added later in HTML. Output a landscape image suitable as a high-quality website banner.
